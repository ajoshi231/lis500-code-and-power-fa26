A First Timer's Primer to Implicit Bias, the IAT Test, and Intersectionality

About This Guide

This guide is about learning how implicit biases can influence a person's decision-making and associations. It includes tools such as the Harvard Implicit Association Test (IAT) and intersectionality questions to help people recognize and reflect on their own biases.

Implicit Bias

What is implicit bias? Implicit bias is an unconscious or automatic association toward a person or group that can influence how we think, make decisions, or behave without us realizing it.

Intersectionality

What is intersectionality? Intersectionality is the idea that a person's different identities, like gender, race, or class, can overlap and shape their experiences. This overlap of identities can also determine how an individual experiences discrimination or privilege.

Questions to Consider

  • How can implicit bias affect how we interact with others for the first time?
  • Why might it be important to recognize what our own biases are?
  • How can first impressions be affected by implicit bias?
  • Could learning about people's different experiences challenge our own biases and make us think differently?
