Why Does This Matter? Maybe You Can't See Me.

In Unmasking AI, Joy Buolamwini's work exemplifies how technology that appears to be all-knowing and objective on paper can instead propagate inequalities and biases. Buolamwini encapsulates the gist of her research in her spoken-word poem, AI, Ain't I A Woman?, which illustrates facial recognition algorithms misreading the countenances of famous Black women like Serena Williams and Sojourner Truth.

Want more backstory? Dig in: MIT Black History Project page for AI, Ain't I A Woman?
