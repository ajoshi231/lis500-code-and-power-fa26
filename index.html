Why Does This Matter? Maybe you can't see me.

In Unmasking AI, Joy Buolamwini's work exemplifies how technology that appears to be all-knowing and objectives on paper can instead propagate inequalities and biases. Buolamwini turned her research into a spoken word poem, AI, Ain't I A Woman?, which depicts facial recognition algorithms misreading the countenances of famous Black women like Serena Williams.

Want more backstory? Dig in: MIT Black History Project page for AI, Ain't I A Woman?
